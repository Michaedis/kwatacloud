Questions

Before you share a drop link.

Do fans install an app?

No. You send a link. They hear 30 seconds in the browser, then pay with mobile money.

Which files can I sell?

WAV, FLAC, and AIFF. The preview is made from that master. The buyer downloads the file you uploaded.

When does the money arrive?

This sample shows an instant MTN MoMo or Orange Money settlement. It is not connected to a wallet, so nothing arrives.

Can I sell merch without a track?

Yes. A drop can be a master, a merch run, or both on the same page.

Where do the payout rails apply?

The sample names MTN MoMo and Orange Money, which fans already use across West, Central, and East Africa. A city on a drop is only a label here.

Is this a real store?

No. kwatacloud is a sample interface for layout and flow. Nothing is uploaded, charged, or paid out.
